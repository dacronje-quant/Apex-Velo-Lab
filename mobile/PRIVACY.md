# Apex Velo for Android: privacy policy

Apex Velo is a cycling training app. It runs on your phone and has no server or account of its own.

## Health Connect

With your permission (Settings > Health, one permission per data type, each of which you can refuse or remove in Health Connect at any time), Apex Velo:

- **reads** heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and its stages, weight, blood oxygen and breathing rate, to calculate your daily readiness and show recovery trends;
- **reads** exercise sessions and, for cycling sessions only, the heart rate, power, speed, cadence and distance recorded during them, to add rides from other apps to your training history;
- **writes**, only if you switch it on, the rides you record in Apex Velo (session, heart rate, power, cadence, speed, distance and calories);
- reads past data (up to two years) once, then only what changed, and only while the app is open.

## Where your data goes

- Health Connect data, rides and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and in Android's backup of the app if your phone backs up apps.
- Apex Velo does not send health data to the developer or anyone else, does not use it for advertising, and never sells it.
- Data leaves the phone only when you ask for it: rides you upload to Strava or intervals.icu, files you export or share, and the training summary sent to an AI provider when you use the AI coach with your own API key.

## Removing your data

Disconnect Health Connect in Settings > Health, remove the app's permissions in Health Connect, or uninstall the app (which deletes everything it stored on the phone; FIT files saved to Documents/ApexVelo stay until you delete them).
