Pedal error vs target: --
⚡ BLUETOOTH CONNECTION TROUBLESHOOTER
  • Wake Up Pedals: Spin cranks backwards 3–4 times. Assioma LEDs will flash (pedals sleep after 2m).
  • Disconnect Phone: Most sensors accept only one or two connections. Force-close the Favero Assioma app, Wahoo app, Polar Beat/Flow or Zwift on your phone/tablet.
  • Polar H10: Wet the electrodes and wear the strap (it only wakes on skin contact). If it was ever paired in Windows Settings > Bluetooth, remove it there, then connect from here. Failed first attempts are retried automatically.
  • Windows Bluetooth: Ensure Bluetooth is toggled ON in Windows Settings / Action Center.
  • Browser Popup: When you click Connect, look for the device popup at the top-left under the address bar and click Pair.
  • Use Launcher: For guaranteed permissions, double-click Launch-Apex-Velo.bat in your folder.

RSSI appears only when the browser exposes advertisement data (Chrome experimental Web Bluetooth). A dropped link reconnects automatically with exponential backoff (1 s, 2 s, 4 s … capped at 30 s, 8 attempts).
